# A Bottle has a State, and an open one has a Level

A Bottle is *closed*, *open* or *finished*, and an open Bottle carries an approximate **Level**:
full, ¾, ½, ¼. A finished Bottle is kept.

Wine is usually opened and finished the same evening, so for wine this is two states in one
press. Spirits stay open for months, and *"what is in the house"* means nothing to a sommelier
who cannot tell a full bottle from a last finger. Four coarse steps are what the owner can judge
by eye; anything finer would be invented.

## Consequences

- The Cellar is the Bottles that are not finished.
- Finishing a Bottle does not create a Tasting by itself; the assistant offers to record one.
