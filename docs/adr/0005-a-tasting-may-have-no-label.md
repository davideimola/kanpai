# A Tasting may have no Label

A Tasting records an occasion and an opinion, and it **may point at no Vintage at all**. In that
case it carries a free description — Kind, grape, region, *"house red"* — instead.

At a restaurant, by the glass, the producer is often unknown and nobody wants to ask. Refusing
those Tastings would throw away a large share of what the owner actually drinks, and for a
beginner they carry as much about taste as a catalogued bottle does: *"liked the Primitivo, found
the Nebbiolo too dry"* is exactly what the Palate is built from.

## Consequences

- A loose Tasting can be **attached to a Label later**, if one turns up; nothing else about it
  changes.
- The sommelier reads loose Tastings alongside the others; queries that aggregate by Label simply
  do not see them.
